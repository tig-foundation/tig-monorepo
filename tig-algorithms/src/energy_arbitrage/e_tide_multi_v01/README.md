## Method

Deterministic online battery-network policy for energy arbitrage under fuel.
Seed portfolio, projected gradient polish, and budgeted joint pair local search
scored with day-ahead DP continuation. Multiday track is the competitive focus;
dense and capstone use a plain path by design (public-minimal surface).

Competitive quality is empirical on library and fuel SO (seed gnosis_replay_v1).
